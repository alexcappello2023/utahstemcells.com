## When thinning hair starts to change how you feel

Noticing more scalp in the mirror, a widening part, or hair that just does not feel as full as it used to can be quietly frustrating — for men and women alike. Many people want to do something before it progresses further, but are not ready for surgery or a transplant. There is a natural, non-surgical option worth understanding first.

## Regenerative hair regrowth using your own PRP

Our hair restoration treatments use your body's own platelet-rich plasma (PRP), with or without stem cells, to encourage natural hair growth by supporting the follicles you already have — no surgery or transplant required. PRP is prepared from a small sample of your own blood and concentrated for its growth factors, which are designed to nourish existing follicles. Each treatment plan also includes a complimentary [microneedling](/microneedling/) session to support absorption and results.

## Who it's for and what to expect

Hair restoration tends to suit men and women experiencing thinning or early [hair loss](/hair-loss/) who want a natural, minimally invasive option. Candidacy is confirmed by the physician, who evaluates your pattern of hair loss during a consultation.

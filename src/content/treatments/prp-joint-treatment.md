## What is PRP?

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) is prepared from a small sample of your own blood, concentrated to increase the growth factors that support healing, then injected precisely into the affected joint.

## Why patients choose PRP

- Uses your own biology — no donor material
- In-office procedure with minimal downtime
- Often combined with a broader regenerative plan

## The process

After a physician evaluation, we draw a small blood sample, prepare the PRP, and administer the injection under sterile, guided conditions.

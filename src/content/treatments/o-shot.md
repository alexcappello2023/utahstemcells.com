## Intimate wellness is part of overall wellness

Changes in intimate comfort, sensitivity, or confidence are common — and they're rarely talked about openly. Many women quietly live with dryness, reduced sensation, or a sense that things simply aren't what they used to be, unsure of what options exist or who to ask. You deserve a discreet, respectful place to have that conversation with a physician.

## What the O-Shot is and how it works

The O-Shot is a physician-performed, in-office injection that uses your own **platelet-rich plasma (PRP)**. The process is straightforward:

1. **Blood draw.** A small sample of your blood is taken, much like a routine lab draw.
2. **PRP preparation.** The sample is processed to concentrate your platelets into PRP, which is rich in natural growth factors.
3. **Targeted placement.** After a topical numbing preparation for comfort, the physician places the PRP into specific intimate areas.
4. **Natural response.** Over the following weeks, the growth factors may support your body's own tissue and circulation responses.

Because the treatment draws on your own biology, it uses no synthetic fillers or medications.

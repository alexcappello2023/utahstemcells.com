## When your energy, mood and drive don't feel like your own

Fatigue that sleep does not fix, a low mood, changes in drive or focus — as hormone levels shift with age, many men and women notice they simply do not feel like themselves. It can be hard to know whether hormones are part of the picture. That is exactly what lab work and a physician evaluation are for.

## Steady, long-lasting hormone optimization

Hormone pellet therapy uses tiny bioidentical pellets placed just under the skin that release hormones steadily over several months, helping to balance energy, mood and overall wellness — without the peaks and valleys of some other methods. Because the pellets release gradually, many patients find the approach more convenient than remembering a daily regimen. Treatment always begins with lab work to evaluate your hormone levels, so your plan is tailored to you and monitored over time. As part of a broader optimization conversation, some patients also explore [peptide therapy](/peptide-therapy/), which the physician can discuss where it may be appropriate.

## Who it's for and what to expect

Hormone pellet therapy — a form of bioidentical hormone replacement therapy (HRT) — is available for both men and women following a physician evaluation, and for men that can include testosterone optimization when labs support it. Candidacy is not assumed: your plan begins with lab work to evaluate your hormone levels, and the physician reviews your goals and health history to determine whether the therapy is appropriate for you. When it is, the pellet is placed just under the skin during a brief in-office procedure and releases steadily over several months, so treatment is repeated periodically rather than daily. Individual results vary, and the physician sets honest, realistic expectations for your specific situation.

## Steady, long-lasting hormone optimization

Hormone pellet therapy uses tiny pellets placed just under the skin that release hormones steadily over several months, helping to balance energy, mood and overall wellness — without the peaks and valleys of some other methods.

## Personalized and monitored

Treatment begins with lab work to evaluate your hormone levels, so your plan is tailored to you and monitored over time.

## For men and women

Hormone pellet therapy is available for both men and women following a physician evaluation.

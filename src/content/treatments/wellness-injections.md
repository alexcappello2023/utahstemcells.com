## A quick boost when you need one

Some days you just feel a step behind — low on energy, dragging through your routine, wishing for a simple reset that doesn't take an afternoon. A quick, targeted nutrient shot can be an easy way to support how you already take care of yourself.

## Nutrients in minutes

Wellness injections are quick, in-office shots of specific vitamins and nutrients — such as B12 and other nutrient blends — designed to support energy, metabolism and general wellness. Because they're delivered directly, your body has efficient access to what's in the shot, and the whole thing takes only a few minutes.

This is general wellness support, an easy add-on to your routine — not a treatment for any disease.

## Popular options

- **Vitamin B12** — support for everyday energy
- **Nutrient blends** — chosen around your wellness goals
- **Easy add-on** — pairs well with an IV therapy visit or a stand-alone stop

A provider helps you choose the option that fits what you're looking for.

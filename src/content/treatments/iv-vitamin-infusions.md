## A convenient way to top up hydration and nutrients

Long weeks, travel, tough training or just running on empty — sometimes water and a good night's sleep aren't quite enough to feel like yourself again. IV vitamin infusions offer a simple, physician-overseen way to rehydrate and refuel, without guesswork about what your body actually needs.

## How IV vitamin infusions work

An IV vitamin infusion delivers a customized blend of fluids, vitamins and nutrients directly into your bloodstream through a small IV line. Because it bypasses digestion, your body has quick, efficient access to what's in the blend — whether the goal is hydration, everyday energy, immune wellness or recovery. For immune-focused support, blends can include [high-dose vitamin C](/iv-vitamin-c-infusions/).

This is part of our broader [IV therapy](/iv-therapy/) menu, alongside [NAD+ IV therapy](/nad-iv-therapy/) and quick [wellness injections](/wellness-injections/). It's general wellness support — a way to complement how you already take care of yourself — not a treatment for any disease.

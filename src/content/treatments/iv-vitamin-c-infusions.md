## A convenient boost for immune wellness

When you want to support your immune wellness and general vitality — heading into a busy season, bouncing back after travel, or just feeling run-down — an IV vitamin C infusion offers a simple, physician-overseen option. It's a wellness boost, delivered comfortably in-office.

## How IV vitamin C works

An IV vitamin C infusion delivers vitamin C directly into your bloodstream through a small IV line. Because it bypasses digestion, your body can access higher levels than are typically achievable through oral supplements — which is the main reason people choose the IV route for a wellness boost.

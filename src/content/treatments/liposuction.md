## When stubborn fat won't budge

You eat well and stay active, but certain areas just won't respond — a pocket on the abdomen, softness at the flanks, fullness under the chin. Stubborn, localized fat can resist even a consistent diet and exercise routine, leaving a contour that doesn't reflect the work you put in.

## Sculpt the fat, refine the skin

Liposuction is a physician-performed procedure that removes stubborn, localized fat from targeted areas to sculpt a smoother, more refined contour. Because skin doesn't always retract fully once fat is removed, it can be combined with skin tightening to firm the surrounding area for a more even result.

It is important to be clear about what liposuction is and isn't: it is a **contouring** procedure that shapes specific areas — not a weight-loss method. It works best for stubborn pockets of fat in people who are already near a stable, healthy weight. Depending on your goals, the physician may discuss complementary options — using [fat transfer](/fat-transfer/) to restore volume elsewhere, or [radiofrequency body contouring](/rf-body-contouring/) to further tighten the skin without additional surgery.

## Who it's for and what to expect

Good candidates are generally people near a stable weight who are bothered by specific pockets of fat that resist diet and exercise. Every plan begins with a physician evaluation to confirm you are a candidate, identify the right areas, assess your skin quality, and set honest expectations.

The procedure is performed in-office. Some swelling, bruising and tenderness is normal at first, and compression garments are often used to support healing. Recovery timelines vary by the number and size of areas treated, and results vary from person to person. Maintaining a stable weight afterward helps preserve your contour. Your provider will explain exactly what to expect and how to care for the treated areas.

## Restore facial volume and tone — naturally, without surgery

Over time faces can start to look a little flatter or less refreshed, and the areas that once looked full can feel like they've softened. You'd like to look rested and restored — but you want a natural, non-surgical route that works with your own biology rather than against it.

The Vampire Face Lift is built around exactly that idea.

## How the Vampire Face Lift works

The Vampire Face Lift uses platelet-rich plasma (PRP) drawn from a small sample of your own blood — on its own or combined with stem cells — **injected** into targeted areas of the face to help restore volume, tone and a refreshed appearance.

1. **A small blood draw.** We take a small sample and concentrate the platelet-rich plasma, rich in your body's own growth factors.
2. **Targeted injection.** Your PRP — with or without stem cells, based on the physician's evaluation — is injected into the areas being addressed.
3. **Natural response.** Over the following weeks your body responds, supporting facial volume and tone.

Unlike the [Vampire Facial](/vampire-facial/), the Vampire Face Lift **does not involve microneedling** — it's an injectable approach focused on volume and tone.

## Vampire Face Lift vs. Vampire Facial

They share a name but do different things — keep them straight:

- **Vampire Face Lift** — **injectable** PRP, with or without stem cells, to help restore facial **volume and tone**. No microneedling.
- **[Vampire Facial](/vampire-facial/)** — microneedling combined with PRP applied to the skin's **surface** to improve texture and glow.

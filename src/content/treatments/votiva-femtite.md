## Feminine wellness, addressed with care

Changes in intimate comfort and confidence are a natural part of many women's lives — after childbirth, with hormonal shifts, or simply over time. Yet these concerns are rarely discussed openly, and many women aren't sure what non-surgical options exist. You deserve a discreet, respectful place to explore them with a physician.

## What Votiva and FemTite are and how they work

Votiva and FemTite are non-surgical, in-office treatments that use gentle **radiofrequency (heat) energy** to support feminine wellness and comfort. The approach is straightforward:

1. **Evaluation.** The physician reviews your goals and history and confirms whether you're a good candidate.
2. **Gentle radiofrequency.** Controlled heat energy is applied to the tissue in a comfortable, in-office setting.
3. **Natural response.** The warmth may support your body's own tissue and circulation responses over the following weeks.

Because it's non-surgical, there's no operating room and minimal downtime.

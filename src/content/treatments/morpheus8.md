## When you want firmer, tighter skin — not just smoother

As skin matures it can start to feel a little looser along the jawline, the neck or other areas, and surface treatments alone don't always deliver the lift people are hoping for. You want to see firmer, more toned skin — while still looking like yourself, without surgery.

Morpheus8 is designed for exactly that kind of deeper firming.

## How Morpheus8 works

Morpheus8 combines [microneedling](/microneedling/) with radiofrequency (RF) energy. The fine needles create controlled micro-channels while delivering RF heat beneath the surface of the skin. That deeper energy prompts your skin to remodel and build fresh collagen, supporting a firmer, tighter and smoother-looking result over time.

Reaching deeper than microneedling alone is what makes Morpheus8 a go-to when the goal is tightening rather than surface texture by itself.

## Face and body

Morpheus8 can be used on the face and on various areas of the body. Your provider will design a plan based on your goals and the areas you most want to address.

## Morpheus8 for stretch marks

One of the most common questions we hear is whether Morpheus8 can help with **stretch marks**. Because it works by prompting the skin to remodel and build collagen beneath the surface, Morpheus8 is often explored to help improve the appearance of stretch marks and smooth uneven texture on the body. It won't erase them completely, results vary from person to person, and a physician evaluation is the right way to know whether it's a good fit for your skin.

## Who it's for and what to expect

Good candidates are generally people looking to firm and tighten skin without surgery and who are comfortable with a short series of sessions. Every plan begins with a physician evaluation to confirm you're a candidate. Results build gradually as your skin remodels, and your provider will give you clear, realistic expectations for your treatment areas.

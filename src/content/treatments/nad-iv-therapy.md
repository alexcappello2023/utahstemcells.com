## What NAD+ is and how the IV infusion works

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every cell in your body. Your cells rely on it to turn nutrients into usable energy and to support many of the everyday processes that keep you running. Natural NAD+ levels tend to decline over time, which is part of why people explore ways to support them.

NAD+ IV therapy delivers NAD+ **intravenously** — directly into your bloodstream through an IV — so it bypasses digestion and reaches your cells efficiently. The infusion is given slowly for comfort and is **physician-supervised** from start to finish. It's designed as general wellness and energy support, not as a treatment or cure for any medical condition.

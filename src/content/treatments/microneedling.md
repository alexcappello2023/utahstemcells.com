## Smoother, fresher-looking skin — powered by your own renewal

Uneven texture, enlarged-looking pores, dullness, the first fine lines — these are the everyday skin concerns that make people feel their reflection no longer matches how they feel. You don't necessarily want a dramatic overhaul. You want your skin to look like a healthier, more refreshed version of itself.

Microneedling is one of the most versatile ways to encourage exactly that.

## How microneedling works

Microneedling uses a precision device with very fine needles to create controlled micro-channels in the surface of the skin. Your body reads these as signals to repair, kicking off a natural renewal process that supports collagen and fresh, healthier-looking skin.

Because it works **with** your own biology rather than resurfacing aggressively, microneedling can improve texture, tone and the look of fine lines and pores with minimal downtime.

## Rebuild your own collagen

Sculptra works differently from traditional fillers: it encourages your skin to rebuild its own collagen over time, for gradual and natural-looking volume restoration that can last.

## A gradual, natural result

Because results build progressively, Sculptra is a good option for patients who prefer subtle, natural-looking change over a series of sessions.

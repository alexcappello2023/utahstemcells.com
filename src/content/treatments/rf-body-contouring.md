## When firmer contours are the goal

You may be close to the shape you want, but notice areas that have lost firmness — a softening abdomen, laxity along the arms or thighs, skin that no longer snaps back the way it used to. For many people the frustration isn't fat so much as tone: they want smoother, firmer contours without committing to surgery and its recovery.

## Tighten and refine, minimally invasively

Radiofrequency body contouring uses focused energy to gently heat the deeper layers of the skin, encouraging it to tighten and refine over time. With a minimally invasive approach and no large incisions, the physician targets areas such as the abdomen, arms, thighs, flanks, knees and back.

The change develops gradually as the treated tissue responds over the following weeks and months. It is a skin-tightening and contouring treatment — it firms and smooths the areas you have, and is not a weight-loss method. If your main concern is dimpling rather than laxity, ask the physician about [cellulite treatment](/cellulite-treatment/), which is aimed specifically at smoothing that texture.

## Who it's for and what to expect

Good candidates are generally people bothered by mild to moderate skin laxity who want firmer contours without surgery. Every plan begins with a physician evaluation to confirm you are a candidate, identify the right areas, and set honest expectations.

The procedure is performed in-office. Because it is minimally invasive, many patients experience limited downtime, though some redness, swelling or tenderness is normal at first. Skin tightening develops gradually over the following weeks and months, and results vary from person to person. Your provider will explain exactly what to expect for your treatment areas and how to care for the skin afterward.

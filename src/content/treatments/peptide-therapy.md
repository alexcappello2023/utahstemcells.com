## A physician-guided way to support recovery, energy and wellness

Slow recovery, low energy, a sense that your body isn't bouncing back the way it used to — many people look for something beyond the basics to support how they feel and perform. Peptide therapy is one physician-guided option worth understanding, explained honestly rather than hyped.

## What peptides are and how therapy works

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules — in effect, messengers that help direct processes your body already carries out. In peptide therapy, specific peptides are prescribed as part of an **individualized, physician-supervised protocol**, most often given as small subcutaneous injections.

The goal isn't a quick fix. It's a targeted, medically guided way to support goals like recovery, vitality and general wellness — as part of how you already take care of yourself.

## What peptide therapy may support

Different peptides are studied for different goals. Depending on your evaluation, a protocol may focus on areas such as:

- **Recovery & tissue support** — peptides such as BPC-157 and TB-500
- **Energy, vitality & growth-hormone support** — peptides such as Sermorelin, CJC-1295 and Ipamorelin
- **Metabolic & weight support** — often alongside our [medical weight loss](/medical-weight-loss/) program
- **Hormone optimization** — complementing [hormone therapy](/hormone-pellet-therapy/) where appropriate
- **Sexual wellness** — peptides such as PT-141

These are wellness and optimization goals — not treatments or cures for any disease. The physician determines which peptides, if any, are appropriate for you.

## Regenerative support, delivered intravenously

IV stem cell therapy delivers regenerative components through the bloodstream, taking a whole-body approach rather than targeting a single joint. Patients often consider it as part of a broader wellness and recovery plan.

## What to expect

The infusion is administered in-office under physician supervision. Your provider will review your goals and health history during a consultation to determine whether it's appropriate for you.

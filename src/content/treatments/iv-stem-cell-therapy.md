## A whole-body approach to wellness and recovery

Most people first hear about regenerative medicine in the context of a single joint — a worn knee or an aching shoulder. But some patients are looking for something broader: support for how they feel and recover overall, not just relief in one spot. That is the question IV stem cell therapy is meant to explore.

## Regenerative support, delivered intravenously

A joint injection targets one specific area. IV stem cell therapy works differently: regenerative components are delivered through the bloodstream, so it takes a **whole-body, systemic approach** rather than focusing on a single joint. Because it circulates throughout the body, patients often explore it as part of a broader wellness and recovery plan.

Localized injections are typically used for [joint and orthopedic concerns](/conditions/). The intravenous route is what makes a systemic approach possible — and patients ask us about it in connection with a range of systemic and neurological concerns, which you can explore on our [conditions pages](/conditions/). Whether it may be appropriate for you, and what to realistically expect, is something the physician determines on an individual basis.

## Who it's for and what to expect

IV stem cell therapy tends to interest patients who are thinking about their overall wellness and recovery rather than a single injury. Candidacy is not automatic: the physician reviews your goals and medical history during a consultation to determine whether it is appropriate for you. The infusion itself is administered in-office under physician supervision, and your provider will explain what to expect during and after the appointment. Because this is a whole-body approach and individual results vary, the physician sets realistic expectations for your specific situation.

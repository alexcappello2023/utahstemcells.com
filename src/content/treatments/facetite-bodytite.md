## When loose skin doesn't match how you feel

Skin naturally loosens over time — with age, after weight changes, or after pregnancy. You might notice softening along the jawline, a little laxity under the chin, or areas of the abdomen, arms or thighs that no longer feel as firm as they once did. For many people, the gap between full surgery and doing nothing feels frustrating: they want a meaningful change without a facelift or a tummy tuck and the recovery that comes with them.

There is a minimally invasive option worth understanding first.

## Radiofrequency skin tightening, minimally invasive

FaceTite and BodyTite use radiofrequency energy delivered just beneath the skin to gently tighten and refine areas that have lost firmness. Instead of the larger incisions of traditional surgery, the physician works through very small entry points, targeting loose tissue on the face, neck or body.

**FaceTite** focuses on the face and neck — the jowls, jawline, nasolabial folds and the area under the chin. **BodyTite** addresses larger areas such as the abdomen, upper arms, thighs, flanks, knees and back. Both are designed to firm and contour with less downtime than a full surgical lift. For milder laxity, the physician may point you toward less invasive [radiofrequency body contouring](/rf-body-contouring/) instead.

Importantly, these are contouring and skin-tightening procedures — not a weight-loss method. They refine and firm the areas you have, rather than reducing overall body weight.

## Who it's for and what to expect

Good candidates are generally people bothered by mild to moderate skin laxity who want firmer contours without committing to a full surgical lift. Every plan begins with a physician evaluation to confirm you are a candidate, identify the right areas, and set honest expectations.

The procedure is performed in-office. Because it is minimally invasive, many patients experience less downtime than with traditional surgery, though some swelling, bruising or tenderness is normal at first. Skin tightening develops gradually over the following weeks and months, and results vary from person to person. Your provider will explain exactly what to expect for your treatment areas and how to care for the skin afterward.

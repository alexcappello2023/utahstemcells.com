## Bring back the volume that time takes away

As we age, the face doesn't just wrinkle — it deflates. Cheeks flatten, lips thin, smile lines deepen, and areas that were once smooth and full can start to look hollow or tired. You may notice it most in photos or in the mirror at the end of a long day.

Dermal fillers are one of the most versatile ways to restore that lost volume and refresh your contours — subtly, when they're placed with a careful, individualized hand.

## How dermal fillers work

Dermal fillers are injectable gels that add volume and structure back to specific areas of the face. Placed just beneath the skin, they gently lift, contour and smooth — replacing what age, movement and gravity have taken away.

1. **Consultation.** Your provider assesses your facial structure and skin and maps the areas that will benefit.
2. **Product selection.** From our range — Juvéderm, Restylane, Radiesse, Belotero, Versa and Bellafill — the right filler is chosen for each area.
3. **Treatment.** The filler is precisely injected in-office, refining contours in a single visit.

## Who it's for and what to expect

Dermal fillers suit adults who want to restore facial volume, refine contours such as the cheeks and lips, or soften folds — often in a single in-office visit with minimal downtime. If your main concern is fullness beneath the chin rather than lost volume, your provider may instead discuss [Kybella](/kybella/), which targets submental fat to refine the jawline. Some swelling or bruising at the injection sites is common and usually settles within a few days.

Results are natural-looking but temporary: depending on the product and area, they typically last from several months to well over a year before gradually fading, so maintenance keeps your result consistent. Your provider will confirm whether you're a good candidate and set realistic expectations during your consultation.

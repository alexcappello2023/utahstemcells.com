## Say goodbye to the stubborn double chin

Fullness under the chin has a way of showing up in every photo and video call, and for many people it's stubbornly resistant to diet and exercise. It can make you look heavier or older than you feel — and no amount of good angles seems to fix it.

Kybella offers a way to address it directly, without surgery.

## How Kybella works

Kybella is an injectable made with deoxycholic acid — a molecule your body naturally uses to break down dietary fat. When it's injected into the fat beneath the chin, it disrupts those fat cells so your body can gradually clear them away, refining the profile over a series of treatments.

1. **Evaluation.** The physician assesses the under-chin area and confirms whether you're a good candidate.
2. **Treatment.** A series of small injections is placed beneath the chin in-office, taking only minutes.
3. **Gradual refinement.** Over the weeks that follow, the treated fat is cleared and the area is reduced. Most patients need more than one session to reach their goal.

## Who it's for and what to expect

Kybella is designed for adults bothered by moderate to severe fat beneath the chin who prefer a non-surgical option. Because fat cells cleared from the treated area don't return to store fat, the reduction is designed to be lasting once you reach your goal — though the specific cosmetic outcome varies and significant weight gain can still affect the area.

Expect a series of in-office sessions spaced several weeks apart. Swelling beneath the chin is a normal, expected part of the process and can last from several days to a couple of weeks. Your provider will confirm your candidacy, outline how many sessions you may need, and set realistic expectations during your consultation.

## Softer lines, without looking overdone

Maybe it's the frown line between your brows that makes you look tense even when you're relaxed, or the forehead creases and crow's feet that have deepened over the years. You don't want a dramatic, frozen change — you just want to look a little smoother, fresher, and more like yourself on a good day.

Wrinkle-relaxing injectables are one of the simplest ways to get there, when they're done with a light, individualized touch.

## How wrinkle-relaxing injectables work

Botox and Jeuveau are neuromodulators. Many of the lines we notice with age are *dynamic* — they form from years of repeated muscle movement like frowning, squinting and raising the brows. These injectables gently relax the specific muscles beneath those lines, so the skin above them creases less and looks smoother.

1. **Consultation.** Your provider studies how your face moves and maps the areas that will benefit.
2. **Treatment.** A few precise, quick injections are placed in-office, taking only minutes.
3. **Results develop.** The effect appears gradually over the following days, softening lines while you keep your natural expression.

## Who it's for and what to expect

Wrinkle-relaxing injectables are a good fit for adults who want to soften expression lines with a quick, low-commitment treatment and little to no downtime. The session takes only minutes, and results develop over the following days. Many patients pair them with volume-restoring options such as [lip fillers](/lip-fillers/) or, for gradual collagen-building refresh, [Sculptra](/sculptra/), for a more balanced overall result.

It's worth knowing up front that results are temporary — many patients enjoy their result for roughly three to four months before the effect gradually fades, so maintenance treatments keep the look consistent. Your provider will confirm during your consultation whether you're a good candidate and set realistic expectations for your face and goals.

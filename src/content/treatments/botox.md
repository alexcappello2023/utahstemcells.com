## Smooth, refreshed, natural-looking

Wrinkle-relaxing injectables like Botox and Jeuveau soften the fine lines and wrinkles caused by repeated muscle movement, for a smoother and more refreshed appearance.

## Quick and convenient

Treatments are quick, performed in-office, and require little to no downtime. Results develop over the following days.

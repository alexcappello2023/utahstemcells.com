## Understanding osteoarthritis

Osteoarthritis is a degenerative joint condition in which cartilage gradually breaks down, causing pain, stiffness and reduced function. It can affect the knees, hips, shoulders, spine and other joints.

## How we can help

Our regenerative approach supports the joint's natural repair and can help reduce pain — often without surgery. Care always begins with a physician evaluation.

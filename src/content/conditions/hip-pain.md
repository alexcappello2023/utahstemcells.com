## Understanding hip pain

Hip pain often results from [osteoarthritis](/conditions/osteoarthritis/), overuse or injury, and can limit walking, sleep and everyday movement.

## How we can help

Regenerative medicine offers a non-surgical path to support the joint's repair and reduce pain. We start with a physician evaluation to confirm you're a candidate.

## When hip pain limits every step

Hip pain has a way of touching everything you do. The stiffness getting out of bed, the ache that flares climbing stairs, the discomfort that interrupts your sleep, the walks and workouts you start skipping — a worn or injured hip can quietly shrink your world. For many people, the options they're offered feel like extremes: manage it with medication or commit to hip replacement surgery and a long recovery.

There's a middle path worth understanding first.

## A non-surgical, regenerative approach to hip pain

Hip pain often results from [osteoarthritis](/osteoarthritis/), overuse or injury — sources where a regenerative approach may help. Rather than replacing the joint, our physician-led options work **with** the hip you already have, using regenerative biologics (your own PRP plus donor-derived stem cells) to help calm pain and improve mobility, with minimal downtime.

Our [stem cell joint regeneration](/stem-cell-joint-regeneration/) and [PRP joint treatment](/prp-joint-treatment/) are designed to support the joint's natural repair and reduce pain — non-surgically, in-office. Because not every hip is a good candidate, an honest evaluation comes first, and the physician will tell you if a regenerative approach isn't likely to help yours. We also help patients with related concerns such as [knee osteoarthritis](/knee-osteoarthritis/) and [back pain](/back-pain/).

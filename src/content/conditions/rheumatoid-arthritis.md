## Understanding rheumatoid arthritis

Rheumatoid arthritis is an autoimmune condition in which the immune system attacks the joints, causing inflammation, pain and damage over time. It is a chronic condition that is typically co-managed with a rheumatologist.

## How we may help

Some patients explore regenerative approaches to support joint comfort alongside their existing care. Any option is considered only after a physician evaluation and in the context of your overall treatment plan.

## An individualized, physician-led approach

Families sometimes explore regenerative medicine as part of a broader support plan for autism. Because every individual is different, any consideration of treatment begins with a thorough physician evaluation.

## What to know

Regenerative therapy is not a cure for autism, and outcomes vary. We believe in honest, individualized conversations about what may or may not be appropriate — never one-size-fits-all promises.

> This page is for general education only. Regenerative therapies for this condition are not a cure and outcomes vary by individual. Any treatment is provided only after a physician evaluation determines it may be appropriate. Nothing here should be taken as a promise of results.

## A careful, individualized approach

Some people living with multiple sclerosis (MS) explore regenerative options as part of their broader care. Any consideration begins with a physician evaluation and honest discussion.

## What to know

Regenerative therapy is not a cure for MS, and results vary from person to person. We work alongside your existing care and never make promises of specific outcomes.

> This page is for general education only. Regenerative therapies for this condition are not a cure and outcomes vary by individual. Any treatment is provided only after a physician evaluation determines it may be appropriate. Nothing here should be taken as a promise of results.

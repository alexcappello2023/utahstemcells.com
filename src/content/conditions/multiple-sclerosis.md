## Living with MS and weighing your options

Multiple sclerosis affects each person differently, and living with it often means staying informed about every avenue that might support your wellbeing. It is understandable to wonder whether regenerative medicine has a role. We want to meet that question with honesty rather than false hope.

Stem cell therapy is not a cure for MS, and it is not an FDA-approved treatment for this condition. What we can offer is a candid, physician-led conversation about what is known, what is not, and whether anything might be considered alongside your existing care.

## How we approach it

We begin with a physician evaluation. Dr. Cimikoski takes time to understand your history and your goals, and to discuss whether a regenerative option such as [IV stem cell therapy](/iv-stem-cell-therapy/) might be considered as one part of a broader care plan.

Whether it may be appropriate is an individual medical decision made by the physician. This is not a cure, is not FDA-approved for MS, and outcomes are uncertain and vary. We also encourage you to continue working closely with your neurologist and existing care team.

## Why patients choose Utah Stem Cells

- **Physician-led, every time.** Your evaluation is with Dr. William Cimikoski, our Medical Director — not delegated to a technician.
- **Honest and candid.** If a regenerative option is not a good fit, we will say so plainly.
- **Alongside your care.** We work with your existing MS team, never in place of it.
- **No pressure.** Our role is to inform, not to sell.

## What to expect

Everything starts with the evaluation. There is no obligation. The physician will give you realistic, honest expectations — including the possibility that a regenerative option is not appropriate. We will not imply that improvement is expected or typical, because that would not be honest.

## Important information

Stem cell therapy is not an FDA-approved treatment for multiple sclerosis. It is not a cure, it does not treat or reverse the condition, and outcomes are uncertain and vary from person to person. Any information here is educational only. Whether any treatment may be considered is determined solely by the physician after an individual evaluation.

> This page is for general education. It is not medical advice and is not a promise of any particular result. Treatment suitability is determined by the physician based on individual evaluation.

## A careful, honest, physician-led conversation

A heart failure diagnosis is a lot to carry. It's natural to want to understand every option and to ask, "Is there anything else that could help?" That question deserves a calm, honest answer — not a sales pitch, and not false hope.

Heart failure is a serious medical condition that must be managed with your cardiologist. Some patients ask us about regenerative options as part of a broader conversation about their care, and we're glad to have that conversation the right way: openly, and with your existing medical team firmly at the center.

## What regenerative medicine is — and is not — for heart failure

Let's be direct. Regenerative therapy is **not** an FDA-approved treatment for heart failure, and it is **not** a cure. We do not claim that it treats, repairs, or improves heart failure, and outcomes are uncertain and vary from person to person.

What a physician evaluation can offer is clarity: a chance to ask questions, understand what is and isn't known, and learn whether anything may be appropriate to consider as part of a broader plan — always in coordination with your cardiologist. Often, the most honest recommendation is simply to continue the cardiac care you already have.

If you'd like to understand the landscape of regenerative medicine in general, you can read about [IV therapy](/iv-stem-cell-therapy/) — but please read it as education, not as a treatment for heart failure.

## Why patients trust Utah Stem Cells for straight answers

- **Physician-led, physician-owned.** Every conversation is led by Dr. William Cimikoski in our Sandy, Utah clinic — not handed off to a salesperson.
- **Honesty on sensitive conditions.** On something as serious as heart failure, we lead with candor. If regenerative medicine isn't the answer, we say so plainly.
- **Your cardiology team stays central.** We work in coordination with — never in place of — your existing providers.
- **No hype, no pressure.** No guarantees, no urgency, no promises of a cure. Just clear information.

## What to expect

Any conversation begins with a physician evaluation of your history, your goals, and your overall situation. The physician determines whether anything may be appropriate to consider, and sets realistic, honest expectations. Please keep your cardiologist fully informed and continue your prescribed care throughout.

## Important information

Regenerative and stem cell therapies are **not FDA-approved treatments for heart failure**, and they are **not a cure**. Outcomes are uncertain and vary by individual. Nothing on this page is a claim that regenerative medicine treats, repairs, or improves heart failure. This information does not replace your cardiac care. Please continue to follow your cardiologist's guidance, and discuss any option you're considering with your established medical team before making decisions.

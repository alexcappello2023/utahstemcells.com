## A careful, physician-led approach

Heart failure is a serious medical condition that must be managed with your cardiologist. Some patients ask about regenerative options as part of a broader conversation about their care.

## What to know

Regenerative therapy is not a cure for heart failure, and it does not replace your cardiac care. Any consideration is made only after a physician evaluation and in coordination with your existing providers.

> This page is for general education only. Regenerative therapies for this condition are not a cure and outcomes vary by individual. Any treatment is provided only after a physician evaluation determines it may be appropriate. Nothing here should be taken as a promise of results.

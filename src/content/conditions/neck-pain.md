## When neck pain and stiffness won't let go

A stiff, aching neck touches more of your day than you'd expect — the discomfort that builds over hours at a desk, the limited turn when you check your blind spot, the headaches that creep up from tight muscles, the trouble finding a comfortable way to sleep. Over time, chronic neck pain wears on your focus, your mood and the activities you enjoy. For many people, the options offered feel like extremes: mask it with medication or consider neck surgery and a long recovery.

There's a middle path worth understanding first.

## A non-surgical, regenerative approach to neck pain

Some neck pain is related to worn or inflamed cervical joints and the soft tissue around them. For those sources, our physician-led regenerative options are designed to reduce pain and support natural repair — without surgery.

Rather than fusing or operating on anything, this approach works **with** the structures you already have. Our [stem cell joint regeneration](/stem-cell-joint-regeneration/) and [PRP joint treatment](/prp-joint-treatment/) use regenerative biologics (your own PRP plus donor-derived stem cells), delivered in-office, with the goal of calming pain and improving how comfortably you move. Because neck pain has many possible causes, an honest evaluation comes first — and the physician will tell you if a regenerative approach isn't right for yours. We also help patients with related [back pain](/back-pain/) and [shoulder pain](/shoulder-pain/).

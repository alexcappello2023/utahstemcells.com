## An individualized approach

Autoimmune conditions involve the immune system attacking the body's own tissues, and are typically co-managed with a specialist. Some patients explore regenerative options alongside their existing care.

## What to know

Regenerative therapy is not a cure, and outcomes vary. Any option is considered only after a physician evaluation and in the context of your overall treatment plan.

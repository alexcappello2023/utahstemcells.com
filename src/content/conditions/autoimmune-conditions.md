## An honest, individualized approach

Living with an autoimmune condition can be draining — the flares, the fatigue, the endless management. It's completely understandable to wonder whether there's anything else worth exploring. That question deserves a calm, honest answer.

Autoimmune conditions involve the immune system attacking the body's own tissues, and they're typically co-managed with a specialist such as a rheumatologist. Some patients explore regenerative options as part of a broader conversation, alongside their existing care — and we're glad to have that conversation the right way.

## What regenerative medicine is — and is not — for autoimmune conditions

Let's be clear. Regenerative therapy is **not** a cure for autoimmune conditions, and it is **not** an FDA-approved treatment for autoimmune disease. We do not claim that it resolves or reverses autoimmune conditions, and outcomes are uncertain and vary from person to person.

What a physician evaluation can offer is clarity: an opportunity to ask questions, understand what is and isn't known, and learn whether anything may be appropriate to consider as part of a broader plan — always alongside your specialist. If you'd like to understand regenerative medicine in general, you can read about [IV therapy](/iv-stem-cell-therapy/), but please read it as education, not as a treatment for autoimmune disease.

## What to expect

Any conversation begins with a physician evaluation of your history, your current care, and your goals. The physician determines whether anything may be appropriate to consider, and sets realistic, honest expectations. Please keep your specialist fully informed and continue your prescribed care throughout.

## Important information

Regenerative and stem cell therapies are **not FDA-approved treatments for autoimmune conditions**, and they are **not a cure**. Outcomes are uncertain and vary by individual. Nothing on this page is a claim that regenerative medicine treats, resolves, or reverses autoimmune disease. This information does not replace your specialist care. Please continue to follow your specialist's guidance, and discuss any option you're considering with your established medical team before making decisions.

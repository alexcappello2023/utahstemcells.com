## Why hair thins

Hair loss has many causes — genetics, hormones, stress and age among them. Early intervention often gives the best chance of supporting the follicles you still have.

## A regenerative approach

Our hair restoration treatments use your own PRP, with or without stem cells, to encourage natural regrowth without surgery.

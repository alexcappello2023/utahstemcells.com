## When your hair starts to thin

Noticing more hair in the brush, a widening part, or a thinning crown can be quietly unsettling. Hair is tied to how we feel about ourselves, and watching it change is never easy. The good news is that there are natural, non-surgical options worth understanding — and the earlier you look into them, the more you may be able to do.

Hair loss has many causes: genetics, hormones, stress, and age among them. Because the cause matters, understanding what's behind your thinning is the first step.

## A natural, regenerative approach

Our [hair restoration](/hair-restoration/) treatments use your own platelet-rich plasma (PRP), with or without stem cells, to encourage your body's natural regrowth processes — without surgery. Because it uses your own biology, it avoids the risks and recovery time of surgical hair procedures.

To be honest with you: results vary from person to person, and we can't guarantee regrowth. What a regenerative approach may do is help support the follicles you still have and encourage natural regrowth. Early intervention often gives the best chance of a meaningful result, which is why it's worth understanding your options sooner rather than later.

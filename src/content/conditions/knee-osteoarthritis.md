## Living with knee osteoarthritis

Knee [osteoarthritis](/osteoarthritis/) develops as the protective cartilage in the knee wears down over time, leading to pain, stiffness and reduced mobility. It shows up in the small moments — the ache getting up from a chair, the catch on the stairs, the swelling after a long day, the walks and workouts you start cutting short. It's one of the most common reasons people seek joint care, and for many, the options offered feel like extremes: manage it with medication or commit to knee replacement surgery and a long recovery.

There's a middle path worth understanding first.

## A non-surgical, regenerative approach to knee osteoarthritis

Rather than jumping straight to surgery, our physician-led regenerative approach is designed to support the knee's natural repair and reduce pain — working **with** the joint you already have. Our [stem cell joint regeneration](/stem-cell-joint-regeneration/) and [PRP joint treatment](/prp-joint-treatment/) use regenerative biologics (your own PRP plus donor-derived stem cells), delivered in-office, with the goal of calming pain and improving mobility, and helping many patients delay or avoid surgery.

Because osteoarthritis varies in severity, an honest evaluation comes first. The physician reviews your history and imaging to understand how far the wear has progressed, and will tell you if a regenerative approach isn't likely to help your knee.

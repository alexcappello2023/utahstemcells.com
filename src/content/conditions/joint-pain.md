## Understanding joint pain

Joint pain can affect knees, shoulders, hips, the spine and smaller joints, with causes ranging from arthritis to injury and overuse.

## How we can help

Regenerative medicine supports the body's natural repair and can help reduce pain without surgery. Every plan starts with a physician evaluation.

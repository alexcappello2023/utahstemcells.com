## Understanding neuropathy

Neuropathy involves nerve damage that can cause pain, numbness or tingling, often in the hands and feet. Causes vary widely, so evaluation is important.

## How we may help

Some patients explore regenerative options to support nerve-related symptoms. Any approach begins with a physician evaluation, and outcomes vary by individual.

> This page is for general education only. Regenerative therapies for this condition are not a cure and outcomes vary by individual. Any treatment is provided only after a physician evaluation determines it may be appropriate. Nothing here should be taken as a promise of results.

## Living with back pain that won't let up

Chronic back pain has a way of narrowing everything. The morning stiffness that slows you down, the ache that builds through a workday at a desk, the sharp catch when you bend to tie your shoes, the restless nights when you can't find a comfortable position — over time it wears on your energy, your mood and the activities you love. For many people, the options they're offered feel like extremes: mask it with medication or commit to spinal surgery and a long recovery.

There's a middle path worth understanding first.

## A non-surgical, regenerative approach to back pain

Some back pain is related to worn or inflamed spinal joints and the soft tissue around them. For those sources, our physician-led regenerative options are designed to reduce pain and support your body's natural repair — without surgery.

Rather than replacing or fusing anything, this approach works **with** the structures you already have. Our [stem cell joint regeneration](/stem-cell-joint-regeneration/) and [PRP joint treatment](/prp-joint-treatment/) use regenerative biologics (your own PRP plus donor-derived stem cells), delivered in-office, with the goal of calming pain and improving how comfortably you move. Because back pain has many possible causes, an honest evaluation comes first — regenerative medicine isn't right for every kind of back pain, and the physician will tell you if it isn't right for yours. We also help patients with related [neck pain](/neck-pain/) and [shoulder pain](/shoulder-pain/).

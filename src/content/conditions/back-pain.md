## Understanding back pain

Chronic back pain can arise from joint degeneration, disc issues or injury and is one of the most common causes of reduced quality of life.

## How we can help

Our regenerative, non-surgical approaches target the source of joint-related back pain. A physician evaluation determines whether treatment is appropriate for you.

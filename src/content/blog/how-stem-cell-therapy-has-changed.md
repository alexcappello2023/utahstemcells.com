**What are Stem Cells?**

- Stem cells are master cells of the body termed as raw material cells that give rise to other cells with specific functionalities. With the availability of favorable conditions in the body or a lab, stem cells multiply and divide to transform into more daughter cells. They either form self-renewed stem cells or specific ells that control important body functions like brain cells, blood cells, kidney functions, bone, or heart cells. These are the only cells in the body that have the natural ability to give rise to new cells.
- They are foundation cells of the immune and blood system with spectacular properties. They carry the capability to rejuvenate other tissues and cells while also rebuilding and changing into other cell types to replace the damaged ones. A strong source of stem cells is present in umbilical cord blood that is taken during birth and stored for future use. Such is the importance of these stem cells in life-saving treatments.

**Types of Stem Cells:**

There are two major types of stem cells. They are:

**Embryonic Stem Cells**:

These types of stem cells are developed from unused embryos due to the outcome of in vitro fertilization procedure. These types of cells are pluripotent and given to science for research purposes. They can turn into multiple types of cells.

**Adult Stem Cells**:

There are 2 types of adult stem cells.

- One kind of adult stem cell is derived from tissues like skin, brain, and bone marrow with only limited numbers. These types of cells generate further cells about only one specific function. For instance, a stem cell that is derived from the kidney will only give rise to kidney cells.
- Another type of adult stem cell is pluripotent and has been transformed into a laboratory to resemble embryonic stem cells. This procedure was found by researchers in 2006 that lead to further studies. Researches are still going on in this regard to build every type of cell to specific functions.
- These innovations in stem cells and stem cell therapies are aiding in providing treatment to many serious ailments in modern medicine.

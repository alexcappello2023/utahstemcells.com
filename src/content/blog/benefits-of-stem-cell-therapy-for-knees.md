Knee pain is one of the most common reasons patients come to see us — and one of the most rewarding to treat without surgery. For many people, regenerative options can meaningfully reduce pain and improve day-to-day mobility.

## Why the knee is a good candidate

The knee is a workhorse joint that takes decades of load. As cartilage wears, pain and stiffness follow. Regenerative approaches aim to support the joint's natural repair rather than replace the joint entirely.

## What patients often notice

- Less pain during everyday activities
- Improved range of motion
- A return to activities they'd started avoiding

## Is it right for you?

The only way to know is a physician evaluation. We review your history and imaging and give you an honest assessment — including when regenerative care is *not* the right call.

Please understand that at Utah Stem Cells all of our procedures, which involve the use of **Biologic** tissues, are a form of **Prolotherapy**. Prolotherapy is a technique, which was first developed in the 1950s and involves the use of injecting a proliferant solution (irritant solution) into a joint space, ligament, tendon, or other body tissue to stimulate repair and normal function. This technique has been thoroughly vetted through thousands of peer-reviewed publications that have shown significant improvements with this type of methodology to treat many different conditions. Dr. Cimikoski is an expert in the field of Prolotherapy and is board certified by the American Association of Orthopedic Medicine (AAOM) in Interventional Orthopedic Regenerative Medicine (IROM).

Please keep in mind that the regulatory agencies that oversee this practice of Prolotherapy are constantly updating their guidelines and regulations, which practitioners must stay up to date with to remain compliant. At Utah Stem Cells we are always keen on this fact and always remain compliant with all such regulatory changes. Therefore, it is possible that some videos, discussions, or other forms of communication that are posted on our website or other informational materials may reflect services that are no longer offered or have been amended to comply with updated regulations. Any shared results do not reflect the results of every patient and may vary based on age, medical history, and other factors.

Utah Stem Cells is often involved with research studies investigating the usefulness of stem cells and other biologics to improve the symptoms associated with many different musculoskeletal and other body organs.

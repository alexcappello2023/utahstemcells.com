WHAT YOUR RESULT ACTUALLY MEANS

Before you consider the Galleri test, you should know what each result does and does not tell you.

No Cancer Signal Detected does not mean you are cancer-free. It means no recognized signal was found. The test does not detect all cancers, and false negatives occur. Your routine screening matters exactly as much afterward as it did before.

Cancer Signal Detected is not a diagnosis. It calls for confirmatory evaluation through established procedures such as imaging. GRAIL reports that around 62% of detected signals are confirmed as cancer — which also means a meaningful share are not, and that the wait for an answer can be an anxious one.

We would rather you knew all of this before the blood draw than after it.

Book a consultation and the physician will walk you through both outcomes.

What the Galleri test is

A single blood draw that looks for a signal shared by more than 50 types of cancer, and predicts where in the body it is likely to have come from.

What it is not

It is not a replacement for the screening your provider already recommends, and a negative result does not rule out cancer.

Important Safety Information: The Galleri test is recommended for use in adults with an elevated risk for cancer, such as those age 50 or older. The test does not detect all cancers and should be used in addition to routine cancer screening tests recommended by a healthcare provider. Use of the test is not recommended in individuals who are pregnant, 21 years old or younger, or undergoing active cancer treatment. A test result of No Cancer Signal Detected does not rule out cancer. A test result of Cancer Signal Detected requires confirmatory diagnostic evaluation by medically established procedures (e.g., imaging) to confirm cancer. False positive and false negative test results do occur. Rx only. The Galleri test was developed, and its performance characteristics determined, by GRAIL. It has not been cleared or approved by the Food and Drug Administration.
